Frequently Asked Questions

How does the coupon wallet work?

When a dealer weighs your scrap, you can choose COUPON settlement. A digital coupon of exact scrap value is credited to your wallet. You can use it across multiple purchases in the Recycled Marketplace.

Can a dealer reject my request?

If all dealers in your locality are busy, requests remain open until claimed. You can cancel requests easily from your Customer Dashboard prior to dealer arrival.

How are shopkeepers verified?

All shopkeepers must submit their shop address, trade details, and mobile number. Platform administrators personally review and approve each profile before pickup access is granted.

Is digital payment (UPI) supported?

Yes! Shopkeepers can settle via CASH or UPI on the spot, recording the digital confirmation reference in the platform ledger.
